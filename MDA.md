## One-line pitch

Endless runner with a target audience of 18 – 25 year olds with a relaxing vibe and dream like landscape.

## Aesthetics (what the player feels)

- Relaxation
    
- Cosy
    
- Zen
    

## Core mechanics (3 to 5 verbs or systems)

1. Swipe
    
2. Collect
    
3. Score
    

## Dynamics (what emerges when the mechanics meet the player)

- Players swipe sideways to change directions and avoid obstacles.
    
- Players swipe up to jump over obstacles
    
- Players score by progressing through level and collecting collectibles
    

## Progression & content

- **Session length:** 10 minutes per run
    
- **Content in the vertical slice (by Week 6):** 1 playable level, 3 obstacle types and core running jumping and scoring mechanics
    
- **Content by CA3:** More obstacles, environments, power-ups
    

## Platform features (Android)

- **Touch model:** Gesture driven tap and swipe.
    
- **Safe areas and orientation:** Portrait.
    
- **Haptics:** Vibration for haptic feedback to confirm actions in game.
    
- **Lifecycle:** Game running → Focus loss → Pause gameplay → Save state → Player Returns → Resume Gameplay
    
- **Store / testing tracks:** awareness only, no uploads
    

## Performance budget

- **Device:** Samsung Galaxy A05s SM-A057G/DSN, Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 Octa-core processor, Adreno 610, 4GB RAM, Android 14
    
- **Target frame time:** 16.7ms at 60fps,
    
- **Memory ceiling:** under 500mb
    
- **Cold start:** under 2s to boot
    
- **APK size:** under 200mb
