Short Description

A relaxing endless runner where you swipe, jump, collect and explore a dreamlike landscape.


Long Description

Enter a relaxing dreamlike world in this endless runner designed around simple controls and a cosy atmosphere.

Swipe left and right to move around obstacles and swipe up to jump over them. Collect items as you run and build your score by travelling further through the changing landscape.

Features:
- Simple swipe-based controls
- Endless running gameplay
- Jump and dodge obstacles
- Collectibles to discover
- Score based on distance and collected items
- Relaxing, cosy and dreamlike atmosphere
- Designed for portrait play on Android
